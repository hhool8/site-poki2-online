Remaining warnings — reviewed, no action needed

1. "12 titles in the 61–65 char band"

  • Semrush's own "title too long" threshold is 65 characters (or 580 px). All 12 are ≤65 → compliant. Our crawler uses a stricter 60-char line only as an internal caution.

2. "3 short meta descriptions (45/60/66 chars)"

  • False positive: those descriptions contain apostrophes (we've, beginner's, you're). They are fully present in the double-quoted content="…" attribute — browsers and Google parse them in full (111–153 chars). Only a naive regex that treats ' as a delimiter reports them short.

3. "/search redirect chain"

  • Standard Cloudflare Pages 308 normalization (/search → /search/), one hop, applied site-wide to all extensionless URLs. Not a crawl-budget or UX issue.
