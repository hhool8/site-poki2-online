What Is Merge Infinity?

Merge Infinity is an addictive number-merging puzzle game. Tiles with matching values appear on a grid, and your job is to combine them to create ever-larger numbers — eventually pushing toward infinity. The satisfaction of watching small tiles compound into astronomical values keeps every session engaging.

How to Play

Drag or swipe a tile onto an adjacent tile of the same value to merge them into their sum. Plan your moves carefully — the grid fills up quickly, and a clogged board ends the game. Special power tiles occasionally appear; use them strategically to clear space or boost your score.

Controls

  • Drag / Swipe — move a tile to an adjacent matching tile
  • Tap (mobile) — select and place tiles

Tips

Keep your highest-value tile in one corner and build chains toward it. Avoid scattering tiles randomly — concentrated stacks merge faster. When the board gets tight, look for a merge that opens two spaces at once rather than just one.

Spawn Probabilities and Math

Merge Infinity uses a tile-spawn algorithm weighted toward low-value tiles. A 2-tile spawns roughly 60% of the time, a 4-tile 30%, a 8-tile 5%, and larger tiles only 1% each. Understanding these probabilities lets you predict whether the next tile will help or hurt — and adjust your strategy accordingly.

Each merge doubles the value of the new tile. So merging two 8s gives a 16. But merging a 16 and an 8 (different values) is impossible — the game requires same-value merges.

Strategy

Build from the corners outward. Anchor your highest tile in a corner. Build chains radiating outward.

Use space-efficient shapes. Pairs of tiles stacked vertically are easier to merge than tiles scattered randomly.

Watch for the 2-spawn clusters. When many 2-tiles appear simultaneously, you can fast-merge your way to higher tiers quickly.

Plan around the next spawn. The preview shows the next tile. Set yourself up to merge immediately when it appears.

FAQ

Q: What's the highest tile possible?
A: Theoretically infinite — 2048, 4096, 8192, and beyond. Skilled players reach astronomical values.
Q: Can I undo a move?
A: No — plan carefully because every move is permanent.
Q: Is there a time limit?
A: No — you can take as long as you need between moves.
Q: Does the game save automatically?
A: Yes — current board state is preserved across browser sessions.
