What Is Blocky Hunter?

Blocky Hunter is a first-person zombie survival shooter with blocky, Minecraft-inspired visuals. Fight through waves of undead enemies including special boss zombies, manage your ammo, and survive as long as possible.

How to Play

Use WASD to move and the mouse to aim and shoot. Headshots deal critical damage and conserve ammo significantly. Survive each wave to earn upgrade currency — spend it on new weapons, increased fire rate, or larger ammo capacity between waves.

Controls

  • WASD — move
  • Mouse — aim
  • Left Click — shoot
  • R — reload

Tips

Always prioritise headshots — they deal double damage and stretch your ammo through many more waves. Kite enemies in circles rather than standing still; a moving target is much harder to hit in waves with ranged zombie types.

Enemy Types and Wave Progression

Blocky Hunter mixes multiple zombie types in each wave, with composition varying based on the round:

Walkers. Slow, low-damage zombies that spawn in groups of 5–10. Good practice targets for headshot training.

Runners. Fast zombies that close distance quickly. They lack health but can overwhelm you in groups if you stand still.

Crawlers. Disable zombies that grab your legs when close — they don't do damage but slow you down, making you vulnerable to other zombies.

Shooters. Ranged zombies that fire projectiles. They appear from wave 5 onwards and force you to use cover.

Bosses. One boss spawns every 5 waves — large zombies with multiple attacks and high health. Each boss has unique mechanics.

Loadout Progression

Between waves you spend earned currency on upgrades. Three categories compete for your coins:

Weapons. Better guns increase damage per shot. The most expensive weapons are worth saving for mid-game — early waves can be cleared with starter guns.

Survivability. Extra health and faster regeneration matter against crawlers and groups.

Reload and ammo capacity. Larger magazines reduce downtime between kills, which compounds over 20-wave runs.

The optimal strategy is to balance — one fully maxed stat doesn't help if you're dying in early waves.

Map Tactics

Use the corners. L-shaped maps let you funnel runners into chokepoints where their numbers become a liability, not an advantage.

Listen for audio cues. Boss zombies have distinct footstep patterns — hearing them tells you to reposition before they appear.

Save grenades for bosses. Most boss patterns have a vulnerable window after big attacks. Throwing grenades during that window is essential for the high-tier boss kills.

FAQ

Q: Is Blocky Hunter free?
A: Yes — fully free with no microtransactions on Poki2.
Q: Can I play co-op?
A: This version is single-player only. Some clones offer 2-player online co-op, but the Poki2 version does not.
Q: How many waves are in endless mode?
A: Practically unlimited — the difficulty continues escalating, with new enemy combinations every 5 waves. Top players reach wave 80+.
Q: Do you keep upgrades after dying?
A: No — dying resets all upgrades. The goal is to survive each full run as long as possible.
