What Is Glide In?

Glide In is a precision action game where you guide a gliding object through increasingly narrow gaps and obstacle courses. Each level demands sharp timing and steady control — move too fast and you clip a wall, too slow and you miss the window. The simple one-input mechanic hides a steep difficulty curve that keeps you coming back for one more attempt.

How to Play

Click or tap to control the direction and angle of your glider. Navigate through gaps in walls and barriers without touching the edges. The gaps get smaller and the patterns more complex as you progress. React quickly but smoothly — jerky inputs are harder to correct than steady adjustments.

Controls

  • Click / Tap — control glide direction

Tips

Focus on the centre of each gap rather than the walls surrounding it — aiming for the middle gives you the most margin for error. Take a breath between attempts; tense inputs lead to overcorrections. Once you recognize a pattern, commit to your line early rather than adjusting at the last moment.
