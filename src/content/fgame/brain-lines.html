What Is Brain Lines?

Brain Lines is a physics-based drawing puzzle game where you draw lines and shapes to guide a ball to its target. Each level presents a new spatial challenge — draw the right path, ramp, or barrier and watch physics do the rest. It's quick to pick up but increasingly demanding as level complexity grows.

Core Loop & Objectives

Each level starts with a ball and a target (typically a star or goal zone). You draw lines directly on the screen to create ramps, slides, or platforms that redirect the ball toward the target. Complete each level with as few strokes as possible to earn higher ratings. Levels introduce obstacles, gaps, and timing elements that require more creative solutions.

Key Mechanics

  • Draw Lines: Click and drag to draw a line segment anywhere on the level canvas.
  • Physics Simulation: Lines obey gravity and friction — angle and length matter for how the ball bounces or slides.
  • Limited Strokes: Some levels cap how many lines you can draw, pushing you to find efficient solutions.
  • Obstacles: Spikes, moving platforms, and barriers add complexity in later levels.

Strategy Tips

Angle is everything: A shallow ramp sends the ball further; a steep drop builds vertical speed. Think about the exit angle each line produces before drawing.

Use walls: Draw lines against stage edges to create controlled redirects rather than open slopes that overshoot the target.

Fewer strokes = better score: Try to solve each puzzle with one or two lines before adding more. Often the simplest path works best.

Iterate fast: Erase and redraw without penalty in most levels — experimentation is the fastest path to the right solution.

FAQ

Q: Do I need to draw perfectly straight lines?
A: No — freehand lines work fine. The physics engine responds to the general shape and angle, not pixel-perfect geometry.
Q: Can I erase lines after drawing them?
A: Yes — most levels let you clear your drawing and start fresh at any time without penalty.
Q: Does the game save progress?
A: Progress is typically saved in-browser. Clearing cookies may reset completed levels.
