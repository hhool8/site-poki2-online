What Is Pinball Master?

Pinball Master is a physics-driven arcade pinball game wrapped in a bright gem-crushing theme. You fire a ball into a table packed with colourful crystals, monsters, and bumpers, then keep it alive with two flippers while racking up chain combos. Every crystal you smash feeds a score multiplier, and unlocking monsters opens new target zones on the table. It captures the hypnotic rhythm of real pinball — read the ball, set the angle, hit the shot — in a form that runs instantly in any browser.

How to Play

Launch the ball with the plunger, then use the left and right flippers to keep it in play. The ball scores points for every crystal and bumper it touches, and crystal clusters release bonus effects when fully cleared. Losing the ball down the centre drain ends the sequence — you have a limited number of balls per game, so each save matters. Chain shots through the same lane repeatedly to build multipliers before the table resets your combo.

Controls

  • Left / Right Arrow Keys — control the flippers
  • A / L keys — alternative flipper controls

Tips

Trap the ball on a raised flipper to line up aimed shots instead of reacting blindly. Prefer catching over swinging — a controlled catch beats a wild flip almost every time. Watch the ball's shadow on the table surface; it tells you the landing point before the ball arrives.

Strategy

Clear clusters, not singles. Full crystal groups trigger bonuses worth far more than scattered hits.

Protect the centre drain. Most balls are lost between the flippers; nudge timing beats frantic flipping.

Bank shots off the walls. Direct lines are often blocked — side-angle rebounds reach hidden target zones.

FAQ

A: Yes — it runs in your browser with no download or signup.

A: Yes — the portrait table layout is built for touch screens.
Q: How many balls do I get per game?
