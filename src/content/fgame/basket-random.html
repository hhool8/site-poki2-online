What Is Basket Random?

Basket Random is a two-player arcade basketball game built on deliberately wobbly ragdoll physics. Each player controls a pair of spring-limbed characters on a retro pixel court, and the ball obeys physics rather than basketball logic — it bounces off walls, spins when it hits a character, and gets heavier or lighter depending on the round. The whole game is playable with a single key per player: W for player one, the up arrow for player two. First to five points takes the match. Because the controls never change and only the physics does, a match is over in about thirty seconds and immediately restartable — which is exactly why it works as a pass-and-play game on one keyboard.

The game comes from RHM Interactive, the studio behind the "Random" series, and it has been the most-downloaded entry in that family for years: over a million installs on Google Play and a steady update cadence through 2026. This page plays the browser build, so nothing has to be installed.

How to Play

Pick a mode at the start: you can face the CPU or share the keyboard with a friend. Each side fields two characters, and the only input is a jump-and-swing on that single key. Timing the swing matters more than anything else — the stick reaches further if you let the character wind up first, and a swing made while the opponent is already mid-motion lands harder.

Every basket changes the round. The court changes, the ball changes weight, the characters change proportions — longer arms reach further, heavier balls travel differently, some rounds have you shooting from further out. A tactic that wins one point can be useless three points later.

Controls

  • W — player one: jump and swing
  • Up arrow — player two: jump and swing
  • Mouse / touch — tap your side of the screen on touch devices

Tips

Swing on the backswing. Tapping the key repeatedly throws your character off balance. Let the character rock back, then commit — the reach is longer and the shot is cleaner.

Play the wall. A direct shot at the hoop is rarely the best option. A bank shot off the backboard finds angles the other player cannot cover.

Reset your expectations every point. The round that follows a basket is a new puzzle: new court, new ball, new body proportions. Read the first five seconds before committing to a pattern.

Take the cheap point. When the physics is against you, a soft bank shot at an open rim beats a contested power swing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can two people play Basket Random on the same keyboard?

Yes — that is the main way it is played. Player one uses W, player two uses the up arrow, and both characters are on the same screen at the same time. There is no second device and no second window.

Is Basket Random free to play?

Yes. The browser version runs instantly with no download, no account and no trial limit. The mobile app is also free.

Who wins a Basket Random match?

The first side to reach five points wins. Because the round rerolls its physics after every basket, a lead built on one court may not survive the next one.

Does Basket Random work on a phone?

Yes. On touch devices each player uses their own half of the screen. It is playable on a tablet, though a laptop with a physical keyboard is the better two-player setup.
