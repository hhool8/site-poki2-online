What Is Astro Robot Clicker?

Astro Robot Clicker is an idle clicker set in space — click your robot to generate energy, then invest in upgrades that automate production and send your robot on missions to distant planets. The further you explore, the higher the energy multipliers.

How to Play

Click the central robot to earn energy points. Spend points on upgrades in the right panel — passive generators, click multipliers, and planetary missions. Once you have enough automation, the game runs itself and you can focus on deciding which upgrades to prioritise.

Controls

  • Left Click — click robot to earn energy
  • Mouse — navigate upgrade menus

Tips

Invest in passive generators as soon as possible — they compound over time and outpace manual clicking quickly. Complete planetary missions for large one-time energy bonuses that can fund several upgrade tiers at once.

Progression Mechanics in Depth

Astro Robot Clicker uses three resources that interact multiplicatively: energy from clicking, passive income per second, and bonus multipliers unlocked via missions. Upgrades stack: a 4× click multiplier multiplied by a 3× generator multiplier equals 12× effective energy, but only while both upgrade tiers are active.

Planetary missions are time-limited objectives that deliver lump-sum bonuses. The first mission typically pays 100× your current energy rate, making missions the highest leverage investment whenever they unlock.

Strategy

Front-load generator upgrades. Once a generator is producing 5+ per second, manual clicking becomes negligible.

Stack multipliers during event boosts. Some versions have timed global multiplier events — buy the next multiplier tier right before the event ends so it benefits the longest.

Hover for hidden bonuses. Some skins and stages provide hidden passive income bonuses — read the tooltip.

FAQ

Q: Can I reset prestige?
A: Yes — prestige resets progress but adds a permanent multiplier. First prestige isn't usually worth it; aim for around 8 hours of accumulated time.
Q: Does the game earn while closed?
A: Yes — offline income is capped at 8 hours per return session.
Q: How many upgrade tiers are there?
A: Approximately 25 tiers in the main progression, with prestige adding another 10 bonus tiers.
Q: Are microtransactions present?
A: No — fully free-to-play with no pay-to-skip mechanics.
