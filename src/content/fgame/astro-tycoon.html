What Is Astro Tycoon?

Astro Tycoon is a space idle tycoon where you mine minerals from distant planets and reinvest profits to reach farther and farther into the galaxy. Each new planet unlocks rarer minerals and bigger multipliers.

How to Play

Send mining ships to nearby planets to collect minerals automatically. Sell minerals for credits and reinvest in faster ships, larger cargo holds, and new mining tech. Unlock further planets by meeting credit milestones.

Controls

  • Mouse / Click — manage ships and upgrades

Tips

Balance spending between ship speed and cargo capacity — a fast ship with tiny cargo earns less than a slow ship with maximum hold. Always buy the next-tier planet access when you can; new planets multiply income far more than upgrading existing routes.

Progression Loop

Astro Tycoon follows a classic idle-game progression loop: each planet has a tier (1 to 12), and each tier requires exponentially more credits to access. Planets within a tier cost similar amounts to unlock; the multiplier jumps dramatically at the tier threshold itself. This is where the meta-decisions live — do you unlock every planet in the current tier first, or skip to the next tier for the big multiplier?

Ships come in three categories: mining ships (slow but high cargo), explorer ships (medium cargo, can scout new planets), and relay ships (instant teleportation between unlocked planets). High-level players use relay ships to route minerals between distant high-tier planets efficiently.

Advanced Investment Logic

Cost-vs-return on planet unlocks. Each planet unlock has a price tag and a return multiplier. The first planet in a tier is the hardest ROI — by the third planet in a tier, unlocks are 5–10× cheaper and the multipliers stack.

Relay routes between tiers. Once you have 2+ planets in adjacent tiers, build relay connections. A relay-mining pair runs continuously without needing your input.

Cargo vs speed trade-offs. A slow ship with maximum cargo earns more per run than a fast ship with tiny cargo — but only if the orbit time doesn't dominate. For close planets, speed is wasted; for distant planets, slow ships lose too much idle time.

Prestige reset considerations. Astro Tycoon includes a prestige mechanic that resets your progress in exchange for permanent multipliers. First prestige is rarely worth it — the unlocks return very quickly. The optimal first prestige is around 8 hours of accumulated time.

FAQ

Q: Is Astro Tycoon pay-to-win?
A: No — fully free with no ad-skip or boost purchases. You can prestige purely through patience.
Q: How long until you reach the final tier?
A: Approximately 12–16 hours of active play plus overnight idle accumulation for most players.
Q: Does the game keep earning while closed?
A: Yes — your mining ships continue accumulating minerals offline. When you return, the offline earnings cap at 8 hours per session.
Q: Can you reset prestige without losing unlocks?
A: No — prestige resets all planets, ships, and credits in exchange for the multiplier. Plan carefully.
