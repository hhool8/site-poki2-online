What Is Retro Blaster?

Retro Blaster is an old-school arcade shooter in the bullet-hell tradition: you pilot a lone ship against waves of enemies that fill the screen with projectiles. Dodge, weave, and return fire while grabbing power-ups that upgrade your weapon spread, fire rate, and shields. The pixel-art presentation is deliberate homage — right down to the screen shake on big explosions — and the difficulty curve honours the arcade originals: forgiving early waves, merciless late ones.

How to Play

Move your ship with the mouse while it fires continuously. Survive each wave, destroy enemies before they crowd the screen, and collect the power-ups they drop. Weapon upgrades stack, so a well-protected run builds a ship capable of clearing whole formations in seconds. Losing all lives ends the run — your high score is the trophy, and the leaderboard never forgets.

Controls

  • Mouse — move your ship
  • Auto-fire — your weapon fires continuously

Tips

Keep moving in wide, smooth arcs — enemy bullets aim at your position, so constant motion keeps their spread wide and harmless. Prioritise power-up drops over kills when a choice arises; an upgraded ship pays for itself within a wave. Learn enemy spawn points per wave so you are facing the threat as it appears.

Strategy

Dodge in small circles. Tight circular movement threads dense bullet patterns better than long dashes.

Stack the same power-up type. Repeated pickups of one upgrade outperform mixing — a fully spread weapon clears screens.

Save your shield. When equipped, hold it for the wave transition moments when enemies attack from two edges at once.

FAQ

A: Yes — instant browser play, no download and no account.
Q: Does it work on mobile?
A: Touch-drag controls are supported, though mouse play is most precise.
Q: Is there an ending?
A: Waves escalate indefinitely — the game is an endless high-score chase.
Q: Are leaderboards included?
