What Is Blocky Rider?

Blocky Rider is a block-art motorcycle game set in city streets packed with traffic. Lane-split through vehicles, avoid crashes, earn coins, and unlock new bikes. Multiple modes include endless, missions, and time attack.

How to Play

Tilt your phone (mobile) or use arrow keys (desktop) to steer between lanes. Speed increases automatically — your job is to dodge every car and bus. Near-misses award bonus coins. Use coins to unlock faster, more manoeuvrable bikes.

Controls

  • ← / → — change lanes
  • ↑ / ↓ — speed up / slow down

Tips

Look two or three cars ahead, not at the car immediately in front of you. The higher-tier bikes steer faster — unlock the second bike as soon as possible because its reaction time dramatically reduces crash rate.

Game Modes

Blocky Rider includes three distinct modes:

Endless Mode. The classic experience — your bike accelerates continuously, you dodge traffic, and the distance keeps climbing until you crash. High scores persist on a global leaderboard.

Mission Mode. A sequence of objectives (collect X coins, drive Y miles, avoid Z crashes) with rewards for completion. Each mission lasts 1–3 minutes.

Time Attack. Race against the clock to reach a finish line distance, with traffic arranged for maximum challenge. Wins unlock premium bikes.

Lane Strategy

Centre lane is dangerous. Most traffic clusters in the centre and right lanes. Stay on the shoulder (far left) whenever possible.

Don't linger. Standing still in one lane guarantees a crash. If a car is in your lane and changing lanes is blocked, hit the shoulder briefly.

Anticipate trucks. Trucks and buses take up two lanes visually but only block one. Recognise them in advance and avoid the wider footprint.

Use near-miss bonus. Passing within a small margin of a car awards bonus coins without crashing — push limits on clear roads to maximise income.

Bike Unlocks and Upgrades

The bike garage includes 8 bikes, each with different stats: top speed, acceleration, handling agility, and crash tolerance. The starter bike is balanced; later bikes trade one stat for another. The best choice depends on your playstyle — aggressive players benefit from handling, while patient distance runners prefer top speed.

FAQ

Q: Is Blocky Rider free?
A: Yes — completely free to play with no in-app purchases on Poki2.
Q: Can I steer with my phone?
A: Yes — tilt controls work on mobile browsers. Desktop users use arrow keys.
Q: Are there different environments?
A: Yes — city streets, highways, tunnels, and night-time variants. Each has slightly different traffic patterns.
Q: Does progress save across browsers?
A: No — progress is stored in browser localStorage. Same browser, same machine, progress persists.
