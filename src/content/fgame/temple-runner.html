What Is Temple Runner?

Temple Runner is a classic endless runner set in ancient temple ruins. You sprint automatically along a winding path, swiping or pressing keys to turn, jump over gaps, and slide under obstacles. Collect coins as you run to unlock power-ups and characters. The further you get, the faster the pace — see how long you can survive before a trap ends your run.

How to Play

The character runs forward automatically. Swipe left or right (or press the arrow keys) to turn at junctions. Swipe up to jump over roots and barriers; swipe down to slide under low obstacles. Collect coins along the path and use them between runs to buy upgrades. Each run starts slower and accelerates as your distance increases.

Controls

  • Left / Right Arrow (or Swipe) — turn at junctions
  • Up Arrow (or Swipe Up) — jump
  • Down Arrow (or Swipe Down) — slide

Tips

Always look one or two turns ahead — the turn prompts appear early enough to react if you're watching. Prioritise coin magnets and score multipliers over speed boosts in the upgrade shop; they compound across every run far more than raw speed increases.

Power-Up Strategy

Temple Runner offers four primary power-ups: Coin Magnet, Score Multiplier, Shield, and Head Start. Most experienced players prioritise the Coin Magnet first — coins compound across runs and fund permanent upgrades. Score Multiplier comes second for leaderboard pushing. Shield is situational — powerful during high-speed sections where any collision ends the run.

The Head Start is the rarest power-up. It teleports you past the first 200 m of obstacles, giving an early jump on what would be a slow starting run.

Character Roster

Each character has a unique starting ability: Guy Dangerous starts with a coin magnet, Isla Von Cheeks gets a score multiplier, Mont Simmons starts with a shield. Choosing the right character for your style removes the early-run power-up lottery.

FAQ

Q: How long is one run?
A: Theoretically unlimited; the world record exceeds 50,000 m. Casual players typically reach 1,000–3,000 m.
Q: Are there multiple environments?
A: Yes — the original temple, a forest, a snowy mountain, and a dark cave. Each has unique obstacle patterns.
Q: Can I play offline?
A: Yes — fully playable without internet.
Q: Are there teams or guilds?
A: No — it's a single-player experience.

Coin Route Patterns

Coin runs follow fixed patterns based on the level theme. On the temple theme, coins cluster around turns; on the forest theme, coins form diagonal arcs between trees. Memorise the coin clusters and your per-run score climbs steadily without sacrificing distance runs.

Slide-then-jump combos are essential for tight obstacles. Slide under a low branch, immediately jump over the root that follows. Practice this combo until it's muscle memory; it saves about 0.5 seconds per obstacle compared to two separate moves, which adds up to hundreds of metres over a long run. The Head Start power-up spawns once every 8–10 runs. When you see the golden "Head Start" icon, treat it as a major event and prep your mental route for the first 200 metres. Many top runs start with a Head Start that other players don't get, so treat it like a rare bonus.

Extra FAQ

Q: Can I unlock all characters with coins alone?
A: Yes — every character is unlockable through coins. Premium currency (gems) is purely cosmetic shortcuts.
Q: Does distance or coin count matter more for leaderboards?
A: Distance is the primary metric. Coins grant in-run multipliers that boost distance score.
