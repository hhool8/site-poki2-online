What Is Bike Xtreme?

Bike Xtreme is an extreme motocross racing game with steep hills, ramps, and trick-based scoring. Complete race courses while pulling off flips and jumps for bonus points — crash too hard and you restart the checkpoint.

How to Play

Accelerate with the gas button (or right arrow), lean forward and backward mid-air with the tilt keys to land cleanly. Hitting a ramp with full speed launches you into flip territory — hold the lean key to rotate and release to land upright.

Controls

  • ← → — accelerate / brake
  • ↑ / ↓ — lean forward / backward

Tips

Land flat or slightly front-heavy to maintain speed after jumps. Rear-heavy landings bounce you off the track. Time your flips conservatively early on — an incomplete rotation means a crash and a checkpoint restart.

Physics and Bike Handling

Bike Xtreme uses a simplified motorcycle physics model. Your rider's weight shifts during jumps by pressing lean keys; forward lean accelerates, rear lean slows and lifts the front wheel. Each bike has different handling profiles — smaller bikes accelerate faster but bounce harder on landings, while heavier bikes are stable but sluggish off ramps.

Tracks are designed around three core challenges: precise landings after big air, tight turns on uneven terrain, and obstacles like rocks and logs that require perfect timing.

Trick Scoring

Flips are scored on rotation count — a clean 360° flip is worth more than a sloppy one. Combine flips with whips (lateral bike-angle changes) and no-handers (letting go of the controls mid-air) for combo bonuses. The best runs include at least one big-air trick per jump without sacrificing landing quality.

Risk vs reward. A double backflip is worth 2× the points of a single backflip, but the timing window for landing is half as wide. Most top players save multi-flips for final jumps.

Air time matters. Some ramps have air-time milestones (3 seconds, 5 seconds, etc.) that trigger bonus currency. Knowing which ramps trigger which bonuses is essential for competitive runs.

Track Tiers and Unlocks

The game has 6 track tiers, each themed (Desert, Mountain, Forest, Beach, Volcano, Snow). Each tier introduces new obstacles and weather effects: Desert has sand pits that slow you mid-jump; Mountain has thin air that affects jump physics; Volcano has flowing lava as an instant-fail obstacle. Tier 6 unlocks the Snow biome with ice-slippery surfaces that demand careful throttle control.

FAQ

Q: Is Bike Xtreme free?
A: Yes — fully free on Poki2 with no in-app purchases.
Q: How do I unlock new bikes?
A: Earn stars by completing tracks within target times. Three stars per track unlocks the next tier's bike.
Q: Are there multiplayer races?
A: Single-player against ghost times only. Multiplayer mode is not included.
Q: What happens if I crash?
A: You restart at the last checkpoint. Crashes cost time but don't cost your progress through the track.
