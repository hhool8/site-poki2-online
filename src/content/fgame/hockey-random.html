What Is Hockey Random?

Hockey Random is a physics-based ice hockey game where the rules change between rounds. Every goal re-rolls the arena, the puck, gravity, friction and the size and shape of the players. One round is a fast, slippery ice sheet; the next might be a beach where the puck is a beach ball, or a forest pitch where it barely rolls at all. Because the situation keeps changing, no strategy survives more than a goal or two — the skill is reading the new conditions fast.

The controls are as small as they come: one key per player, W for player one and the up arrow for player two, where the same press moves your skater and swings the stick. The game was added to the browser catalogue in late August 2026 and has been kept current since, and it joins the same "Random" family as Basket Random — one-button games whose randomness is the point.

How to Play

You pick a team and an opponent, then skate. The puck is a separate object with its own momentum, so you chase it, hit it and try to send it into the net. Because the physics reroll after every goal, your first move of each round should be a test shot: take a touch, see how the puck behaves, then commit.

First to five goals wins. Each side fields two ragdoll skaters, which looks chaotic and plays well, since bodies tumble but the movement is still yours to direct.

Controls

  • W — player one: move and swing the stick
  • Up arrow — player two: move and swing the stick
  • Tap your side of the screen — touch control on phones and tablets

Tips

Test the puck first. A soft touch at the start of a round tells you more than any amount of guessing: heavy pucks need a full swing, light ones need almost nothing.

Bank off the boards. When the direct lane to the net is covered, the angle off the side wall is open. The same shot that misses the goalie from the middle finds the net off the corner.

Slide late when defending. Committed too early, you are simply run around. Cut in front of the shot at the last moment instead.

Stay calm — your opponent cannot. Two-player matches are decided less by reflexes than by who reads the new physics first. Panic costs the goal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can two people play Hockey Random together?

Yes. The local two-player mode shares one keyboard: one player uses W, the other uses the up arrow. It is the same screen, the same puck and the same chaos for both of you.

Is Hockey Random free?

Yes. It plays in the browser with nothing to install and no account to create.

Why does every round look different?

Each goal rerolls the arena, the puck type, gravity and surface friction. Shot power and puck behaviour change with them, so tactics have to be rebuilt constantly.

How many goals are needed to win?

Five. The first player to reach five goals takes the match, and the reset happens immediately — there is no long menu between rounds.
