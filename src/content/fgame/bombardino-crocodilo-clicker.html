What Is Bombardino Crocodilo Clicker?

Bombardino Crocodilo Clicker is an idle clicker built around the most famous Italian Brainrot meme character — a missile-shaped crocodile. Click Bombardino to earn coins, buy passive upgrades, and unlock increasingly absurd character variants as your empire grows.

How to Play

Click the large Bombardino Crocodilo in the centre of the screen to earn coins. Spend coins on upgrades in the right panel — click power boosters, passive income generators, and milestone unlocks. As passive income grows, coins accumulate automatically even when you stop clicking.

Controls

  • Left Click — click Bombardino to earn coins
  • Mouse — navigate upgrade menu

Tips

Buy passive generators as soon as they become available — the compounding income eclipses manual clicking within minutes. Aim for the milestone unlock at each major coin threshold; they multiply all earnings for the rest of the run.

The Italian Brainrot Universe

Bombardino Crocodilo is one of the most viral characters from the 2024 Italian Brainrot meme trend — a missile-crocodile hybrid with a sinister grin and absurd sound effects. The character originated on TikTok in early 2024 and quickly spread to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and the broader meme ecosystem. Bombardino Crocodilo Clicker leans into this absurdist energy, awarding in-game points for each click and unlocking increasingly bizarre character variants as your empire grows.

Each character tier has a different art style — pixel, low-poly, fully rendered, anime — and unique animation. Collecting all variants is a long-term goal for completionists.

Strategy

Click during events. Bombardino occasionally enters "frenzy mode" where every click is worth 10× for 30 seconds. Click as fast as possible during those windows.

Stack passive generators. Once you have 5+ generators running, manual clicking becomes a small fraction of your income.

Aim for milestone unlocks. Each major coin threshold unlocks a new tier with permanent multiplier. Don't slow-spend below milestone thresholds.
