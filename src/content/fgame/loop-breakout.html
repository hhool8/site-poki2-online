What Is Loop Breakout?

Loop Breakout is an escape puzzle game starring Tommy, a determined little prisoner who keeps finding himself locked up in increasingly elaborate cells. Each level is a compact puzzle box of traps, switches, moving platforms, and logic tricks, and the way out is never just the open door. Levels loop back on themselves — paths that look like exits often trigger traps, and dead ends hide the real mechanism. It is a game of observation first, reflexes second.

How to Play

Guide Tommy through each level with the arrow keys or mouse. Study the room before moving: spike traps follow rhythms, pressure plates open distant doors, and some walls are illusions. Reach the exit tile without losing your lives to the traps. Early levels teach one mechanic at a time; later levels stack several at once, so solutions require combining everything you have learned.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys / WASD — move Tommy
  • Mouse — interact with switches and objects

Tips

Watch a full trap cycle before stepping into its path — patience costs two seconds, impatience costs a life. Check every wall for fake sections; the game telegraphs illusions with subtle visual differences. If a level seems impossible, you have missed a switch — retrace your steps systematically.

Strategy

Map the loop. Several levels repeat a room with small changes — remembering what changed is the key to the exit.

Use deaths as information. A lost life reveals exactly what a trap does; treat early failures as scouting.

Move switches in the right order. Some plates cancel others — activating in sequence, not all at once, is often the solution.
