What Is Golf Puzzle?

Golf Puzzle is a mini-golf brain-teaser that blends physics with clever course design. Each hole features bumpers, ramps, teleporters, and moving obstacles that you must account for before swinging. The goal is simple — sink the ball in as few strokes as possible — but the layouts reward careful thinking over brute force.

How to Play

Click and drag away from the ball to set power and direction; a dotted trajectory line shows your predicted shot. Release to hit. Navigate around hazards, use walls as banks, and land the ball in the cup. Par varies per hole, and a lower stroke count earns a higher star rating.

Controls

  • Click + Drag — set shot direction and power
  • Release — swing

Tips

Use the minimum power needed — overshooting bounces the ball out of position. Study moving obstacles and time your shot to pass through gaps. Bank shots off straight walls are more predictable than curved surfaces, so plan routes through flat sections whenever possible.
