What Is Rooftop Rush?

Rooftop Rush is a fast-paced endless runner set across a city skyline. You skate along rooftops, leaping between buildings, sliding under obstacles, and dodging air-conditioning units, antennas, and the occasional police helicopter buzzing overhead. Coins scattered along the route fund upgrades that extend your runs, and the pace climbs relentlessly — the skyline scrolls faster the longer you survive, pushing your reflexes toward their limit.

How to Play

Your skater moves forward automatically. Jump over rooftop gaps and obstacles, slide under low barriers, and switch lanes to collect coin trails. Missing a jump drops you off the skyline and ends the run, so reading the next rooftop early is everything. Banked coins unlock upgrades — longer magnet range, higher jumps, slower speed ramp-up — that make future runs last longer.

Controls

  • Arrow Up / Swipe Up — jump
  • Arrow Down / Swipe Down — slide
  • Arrow Left-Right / Swipe — switch lanes

Tips

Jump late, not early — the game forgives a late takeoff far more than an early one that lands short. Collect coins on straight rooftop sections only; coin trails across gaps are bait for mistimed leaps. Use slides to chain under consecutive barriers instead of jumping each one.

Strategy

Upgrade the magnet first. A wider coin pull builds upgrade currency faster than any other investment — early magnet levels compound.

Memorize the helicopter pattern. It signals before swooping; use the warning beat to line up your next lane change.

Stay centred. Rooftop edges tempt with coin trails, but the middle lanes leave escape options when a gap appears suddenly.

FAQ
