What Is Run 3D?

Run 3D is a fast-paced endless runner set inside a three-dimensional tunnel hurtling through space. Platforms crumble and gaps appear without warning, forcing split-second decisions as you sprint forward at ever-increasing speed. The immersive 3D perspective makes every near-miss feel thrilling.

How to Play

Your character runs automatically. Use the left and right arrow keys (or swipe on mobile) to dodge gaps and switch lanes. Jump over obstacles or gaps that span your lane. Missing a platform sends you tumbling into the void — restart and beat your previous distance record.

Controls

  • ← → Arrow Keys / Swipe — move left or right
  • Space / Up Arrow / Tap — jump

Tips

Stay toward the center of the tunnel so you have room to dodge in both directions. When the tunnel rotates, keep your eyes on the floor tiles ahead rather than the walls — floor gaps are the real threat. Short, controlled hops clear most gaps more safely than long jumps that can overshoot landing tiles.

Tunnel Geometry

Run 3D generates a procedurally twisting tunnel where the floor can rotate 90° at any moment, flipping your perspective. The illusion of depth is achieved by shrinking distant tiles; gaps in the floor are obscured by the perspective, so you must read the floor pattern carefully rather than the visible tile spacing.

Speed scales linearly with distance. By 5,000+ score units the tunnel rotates multiple times per second, demanding near-automatic reflexes.

Strategy

Center-lane default. Stay in the centre of the tunnel to dodge left or right when needed.

Watch for floor changes. The most reliable signal of an upcoming gap is a brief flicker where the floor texture shifts colour.

Don't overshoot landing. Short hops clear most gaps more reliably than long jumps that overshoot landing tiles.

FAQ

Q: How does the perspective work?
A: The tunnel uses a fixed-camera perspective. As you run, the floor slides toward you, creating the illusion of movement.
Q: Can I jump diagonally?
A: No — only straight up. Movement is left or right; jumping is just vertical.
Q: Is there a maximum speed?
A: No cap — the game accelerates forever as long as you survive.
Q: Are there multiplayer races?
A: No — single-player infinite runner.
