What Is Hoop Legends?

Hoop Legends is a fast-paced basketball arcade game that challenges your shooting precision and timing. Flick the ball toward the hoop, thread it through defenders, and rack up combo streaks for massive score bonuses. Unlock legendary players and special courts as you climb the leaderboard.

How to Play

Aim by positioning your cursor or finger above the ball, then flick upward in the direction of the basket. A guide arc appears briefly — match its curve to account for the distance. Consecutive swishes build your combo multiplier. Blocked shots and misses reset your streak, so accuracy beats reckless volume.

Controls

  • Flick / Swipe Up — shoot the ball
  • Mouse / Touch — aim direction and power

Tips

Aim for the back of the rim on long-range shots — the ball is more likely to drop in than bounce out. During moving-basket challenges, wait for the hoop to reach its closest point before releasing. Keeping your combo alive is worth more points than going for flashy trick shots that carry higher miss risk.
