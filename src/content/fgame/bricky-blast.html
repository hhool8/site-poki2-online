What Is Bricky Blast?

Bricky Blast is a block-breaking puzzle game where you aim a ball launcher at colorful brick formations and fire to clear them all. Each stage arranges bricks in unique patterns with varying hit-point values, asking you to find the best angle to maximize chain reactions and clear the board in as few shots as possible.

How to Play

Aim your launcher by moving the mouse or dragging on mobile. Release to fire the ball. The ball bounces off walls and bricks, destroying any brick it strikes enough times. Collect power-ups that drop from broken bricks — multi-ball splits your shot, and blast orbs explode on impact. Clear every brick to advance.

Controls

  • Mouse / Drag — aim the launcher
  • Click / Tap — fire

Tips

Bank shots off side walls to reach bricks tucked in corners. Aim for high-HP bricks first before they drop toward the bottom. Multi-ball power-ups are most valuable when aimed at dense clusters — trigger them early in your shot rather than saving them for last.

Bank Shots and Chain Reactions

Bricky Blast rewards players who think in geometric rebounds. Every wall reflects the ball at a perfectly symmetrical angle (angle of incidence = angle of reflection). By aiming at a point on the side wall, you can bank the ball to a brick cluster you'd otherwise miss. The most efficient runs plan a single ball to clear 5+ bricks per shot by chaining bounces.

Chain reactions occur when a brick's destruction drops a power-up, which then activates and destroys adjacent bricks. Skilled players deliberately hit one brick to trigger a power-up that clears an entire cluster.

Strategy

Hit corner bricks first. These are awkward to reach with direct shots but easy with bank shots.

Save multi-ball for dense clusters. Multi-ball power-ups split one shot into three. Always use them at the densest brick concentration available.

Use the prediction line. Most versions overlay a dotted trajectory. Trust it for the first shot but adapt on rebounds — wall bounces can confuse the prediction engine.

FAQ

Q: How many shots do I get?
A: Three per level. Some levels grant bonus shots for hitting specific bricks.
Q: Are there boss bricks?
A: Yes — gold bricks require multiple hits before destruction. They reward bonus coins.
Q: Does the prediction line account for rebounds?
A: Only the first bounce. Subsequent bounces require you to estimate manually.
Q: Can I skip levels?
A: Yes — a small coin cost lets you skip any level after you've cleared level 10 once.
