What Is Bricky Blast?

Bricky Blast is a block-breaking puzzle game where you aim a ball launcher at colorful brick formations and fire to clear them all. Each stage arranges bricks in unique patterns with varying hit-point values, asking you to find the best angle to maximize chain reactions and clear the board in as few shots as possible.

How to Play

Aim your launcher by moving the mouse or dragging on mobile. Release to fire the ball. The ball bounces off walls and bricks, destroying any brick it strikes enough times. Collect power-ups that drop from broken bricks — multi-ball splits your shot, and blast orbs explode on impact. Clear every brick to advance.

Controls

  • Mouse / Drag — aim the launcher
  • Click / Tap — fire

Tips

Bank shots off side walls to reach bricks tucked in corners. Aim for high-HP bricks first before they drop toward the bottom. Multi-ball power-ups are most valuable when aimed at dense clusters — trigger them early in your shot rather than saving them for last.
