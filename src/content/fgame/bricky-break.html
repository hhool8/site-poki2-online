What Is Bricky Break?

Bricky Break is an arcade brick-breaker — launch a ball to destroy colorful brick formations and collect bonus balls for chain reactions. Each level has a different layout with special bricks that take multiple hits or drop power-ups.

How to Play

Aim your launcher by moving the mouse and click to shoot. The ball ricochets off walls and hits bricks. Collect extra balls on the field to multiply your projectiles. Clear all bricks to complete the level. Some bricks require multiple hits — they are darker in colour.

Controls

  • Mouse — aim direction
  • Left Click — launch balls

Tips

Aim at clusters of bricks in the centre of the field so the ball ricochets multiple times per shot. Always pick up bonus balls — a large multiball volley clears entire columns faster than targeting individual bricks.

The Classics Revisited

Bricky Break is a modern take on the 1970s Atari Breakout, layered with mid-90s Arkanoid-style power-ups. The simple paddle-and-ball mechanic hides depth: ball angle off the paddle is determined by where the paddle is hit, so consistent aim wins over raw speed. The game rewards patient angles over aggressive flailing.

Each stage introduces new brick tier colours: standard (1 hit), reinforced (2 hits), bombs (destroys neighbours), indestructible walls (must route around), and gems (extra points but no destruction). Plan routes around indestructible walls early.

Strategy

Move the paddle while launching. Don't keep the paddle still for the first shot — multi-ball on level 1 splits immediately and you need every paddle position possible.

Aim for high-value bricks. Top-row reinforced bricks and side bombs give the most points. Clear targets at the top first, not the bottom.

Save the laser power-up. The paddle-mounted laser clears entire columns — but only for a few seconds. Hold it for moments when bricks are densest.

Ball Trajectory Patterns

Ball angle on launch is everything in Bricky Break. A ball launched straight up hits nothing twice because a near-vertical ricochet sends it back to the paddle quickly. A ball launched at a shallow angle ricochets four to six times per descent and clears entire brick rows. Aim for shallow angles on your first shot rather than steep verticals.

Indestructible walls force your route into specific corridors. Don't fight them — accept the route they require and clear the bricks to their left and right before clearing the indestructibles' column last. The "laser paddle" power-up has limited duration; save it for the densest column of bricks rather than burning it on a near-empty board. A laser used on a 12-brick column clears more than twenty individual shots combined, especially when chained with multi-ball spawns in adjacent columns.
